Support

GlassTime keeps the time in every city you care about in a floating glass widget that docks below the menu bar or floats anywhere on the desktop. It requires macOS 26 or later.

The widget is locked and won't respond to clicks

A locked widget lets clicks pass through to the app underneath. To use it again, do any of these:

  • Press ⌃⌥L.
  • Hold ⌥ to interact with it temporarily.
  • Rest the pointer on it for a second until an Unlock button appears.
  • Click the GlassTime icon in the menu bar and turn off Lock Position.

The widget disappeared

It may be hidden. A timed hide (1 or 4 hours) ends on its own. To bring it back now, press ⌃⌥W, choose Show Widget from the menu-bar icon, or open GlassTime again.

Seeing the time somewhere else later today

Scroll over the widget to move through the day in 15-minute steps, or drag along the 24h Ribbon style's timeline. In Settings › Controls you can require a modifier key for scrolling.

Changing the keyboard shortcuts

Open Settings › Controls and record new shortcuts for locking and for showing or hiding the widget.

Starting GlassTime at login

Turn on Launch at login in Settings › General. If macOS asks, allow GlassTime in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions.

Still need help?

Open an issue at github.com/0xnoahzhu/glasstime-site/issues and describe what you see, your macOS version and your GlassTime version (with Settings open, choose GlassTime › About GlassTime in the menu bar).
